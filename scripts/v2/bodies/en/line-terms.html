2. What the service does

  • AI replies: when your customers message you on LINE, the AI answers from the AI knowledge base you approve (Q&A, website, files, today's notices, and live data if you add it). When sources disagree, your approved Q&A wins.
  • Hand-off: for quotes, contracts, payments, complaints, anything the AI isn't sure about, or when a customer asks for a person, the AI tells the customer the owner has been notified and emails you the conversation. You reply from the LINE Official Account app or chat.line.biz.

  • Setup and tune-ups: on Starter you set it up yourself in our wizard. On Light and Standard we write the Q&A, test it with you and launch it, then tune it from real chats every month (up to 5 Q&A fixes a month on Light). Q&A counts, website pages and other limits are as shown on the plans page when you pay.

3. Limits of the AI

The AI writes replies automatically from your information. It is not a person. We work to keep it to what's in your information and to hand off when it isn't sure, but it can still be wrong, incomplete, or misread a customer. So:

  • Check your Q&A and test results before launch. If you spot a wrong answer after launch, tell us or fix it in the dashboard, and we'll correct it promptly.
  • The AI's replies are not promises from us to your customers. Prices, stock, bookings and returns are always subject to your confirmation.
  • We don't guarantee any particular number of inquiries, sales, conversion rate or other result.
  • The service relies on third parties such as LINE, AI model providers and hosting. We keep it running reliably but can't promise it will never be interrupted. If a third party goes down, we restore service as quickly as we can and let you know.

8. Data and privacy

What we store: messages your customers send and the AI's replies, customers' LINE user IDs and display names, hand-off records, monthly active contact counts, your AI knowledge base, and billing records.

What we use it for: only to provide this service: writing replies, sending hand-off emails, showing conversations and leads in your dashboard, calculating usage and fees, improving your Q&A, and fixing problems. To write a reply, the relevant message content is sent to the AI model provider we use. Data is stored with our database and hosting providers (listed in our privacy policy).

What we don't do: we don't sell data, use it for advertising, use your customers' conversations to serve other clients, or contact your customers ourselves.

Roles: your customers' personal data is collected by you for your business; we process it on your behalf and protect it with reasonable security measures. If a customer asks you to see or delete their conversation, tell us and we'll help.

Retention: while you subscribe, conversations stay in your dashboard. Within 90 days after cancellation takes effect we delete your conversations and AI knowledge base; before then you can ask for an export, or ask us to delete them sooner. Billing records are kept as long as the law requires.

Everything else follows our privacy policy.

If we see obvious flooding, bots or attack traffic, we may limit or pause replies for a while and will tell you. Contacts we confirm were that kind of abnormal traffic don't count toward overage.
