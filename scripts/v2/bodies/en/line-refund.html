Refunds and cancellation

Starter, Light and Standard are billed monthly and you can cancel any time. Here's how to cancel, what happens next, and which fees can be refunded.

This page covers the Starter, Light and Standard plans and add-ons bought online, and is part of the terms of service. USD subscriptions are billed by QY LLC through Stripe; NT$ subscriptions with 夏裕科技股份有限公司 follow the Chinese version. Enterprise follows its signed 12-month contract.

How to cancel

Email yufan@shiptolive.com or message us on LINE at @680uqtou and tell us which account to cancel. We confirm within 1–2 business days and tell you the last day of service. You don't need to give a reason, and there's no cancellation fee.

What happens after you cancel

  • The AI keeps answering until the paid period ends. For example, if you're billed on the 5th and cancel on November 20, the AI answers until December 4.
  • No further charges. Your card won't be charged again. Any overage from your last period is settled once, after it ends.
  • We disconnect the AI. When the period ends we stop the AI replies. On request we also remove our admin access from your LINE Official Account, or you can remove us yourself on its permissions page.
  • Your LINE Official Account stays yours, with its friends and chat history. You can keep replying from the LINE Official Account app.
  • Your data: within 90 days after cancellation takes effect we delete your conversations and AI knowledge base. Before then you can ask for an export of your Q&A and conversations, or ask us to delete them sooner.

Monthly fee

Each billing period runs one month from your payment date. Once a period has started, its fee isn't refunded or pro-rated; cancelling stops the next charge. Downgrades also take effect from the next period.

Setup fee (Light and Standard)

The setup fee pays for writing your Q&A, setting up your LINE Official Account and the AI, and testing with you. It's charged once, with your first payment. Starter has no setup fee.

  • Cancel before we start setup: the setup fee and the first month are refunded in full. Setup starts when you submit the onboarding form or add us as an admin of your LINE Official Account, whichever comes first.
  • Once setup has started, the setup fee isn't refundable, because we've begun work on your information. The first month follows the monthly-fee rule above.
  • If we can't finish setup for reasons on our side (for example we technically can't connect to your account), the setup fee and the first month are refunded in full.

Overage

Overage is counted per calendar month (Taipei time) and charged with your next payment. There's none in your first month live, and the monthly total never goes above the next plan's fee. Because it's billed for use that has already happened, it isn't refunded once billed. If you think a count is wrong (for example because of flooding or bot traffic), tell us within 30 days of the charge and we'll check and correct it.

Duplicate or wrong charges

If you're charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or charged after your cancellation took effect, we refund the extra in full. Email us the date and amount.

Offers and discount codes

What a launch offer or discount code covers, and for how long, is as shown at checkout. Discounts have no cash value. Refunds are based on what you actually paid (for example, a half-price setup fee is refunded at the half price).

Consumers: 7-day window

Most of our clients are businesses. If you subscribe as a consumer, for personal and non-business use, the consumer law where you live may give you rights these terms can't take away, and we follow it. We give every consumer the same 7-day window:

  • Monthly fee: tell us in writing (email is fine) within 7 days after the service starts (on Starter, from your payment date; on Light and Standard, from the day the AI goes live), and we refund the first month in full and stop the AI.
  • Setup fee: setup is work made to your request (your Q&A, your account). Before we start, we tell you and ask you to confirm that setup can begin; once you confirm and submit the onboarding form, the setup fee is no longer covered by the 7-day window. Cancel before then and it's refunded in full.
  • For consumers in Taiwan, this follows Article 19 of the Consumer Protection Act (7 days to withdraw from an online purchase without giving a reason) and the exception for "customized performance made at the consumer's request" in Article 2(2) of the Regulations on Reasonable Exceptions to the Right of Rescission in Distance Transactions, which applies only when the consumer is told beforehand. We refund within 15 days of your notice (Article 19-2).

If a regulator or court decides a fee is still covered, we follow that decision.

How refunds are paid

  • We process an approved refund within 7 business days.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe. Your bank usually shows it within 5–10 business days.
