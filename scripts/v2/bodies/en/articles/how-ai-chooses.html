What steps does AI take to answer a question?

An AI assistant with web search doesn't recite an answer from memory. It looks things up online first, then summarizes. Say a customer asks "which accounting firms do you recommend in my city?" The process goes roughly like this:

  1. Understand the question. What service the customer wants, in which area, and with what conditions.
  2. Search. Find relevant pages online. If the AI can't read your website at this step, you won't appear later.
  3. Read and compare. From what it found, compare which businesses fit the question best, for example by services, area and reviews.
  4. Write the answer. Pick a few businesses to recommend and list the websites it used.

Why do some good businesses not get recommended?

Often it isn't because their service is bad. The AI can't find them, can't understand them, or doesn't have enough information to be sure. These are the cases we see most in our checks:

  • Can't read it: the website blocks AI or search engine crawlers, or key pages aren't indexed.
  • Can't understand it: the site shows its text as images or animations, or the service description is all adjectives and no facts.
  • Details don't match: the business name, address and phone number differ between the website, the business profile and directories, so the AI can't be sure it's the same business.
  • No one mentions it: apart from its own website, almost nothing online mentions the business.
  • Answers the wrong questions: the website says what the business wants to say, not what customers ask. Customers ask "how much?", "how long?" and "is this right for me?", and the website doesn't answer.

What can a business do to get recommended?

  1. Measure first. Ask AI 5 questions your customers would ask. See whether it names you, who it recommends and which websites it cites.
  2. Add website content. For each question where you aren't named, add clear service details and Q&A. See how to get ChatGPT to recommend your business.
  3. Add outside sources. Get listed on the directories and platforms AI cites often, and keep your details consistent.
  4. Re-check on a schedule. Every month, ask the same questions again and compare.

FAQ

Why does the AI's list of recommendations change from one time to the next?

AI answers change with the wording, the time, the platform and what the AI finds when it searches. For the same question, ChatGPT and Claude may recommend different businesses. So check with the same set of questions on a regular schedule and look at the trend, not a single result.

Does AI trust my own website or third-party sites more?

It uses both. Your website says what you do and where you serve. Third-party sites, such as business profiles, directories, reviews and news coverage, help the AI confirm that you really exist and how people rate you. If only your own website says you're good, that carries little weight.

What are the most common reasons a website isn't cited?

Crawlers are blocked or pages aren't indexed, key content is only in images, service descriptions are too vague to answer specific questions, the business name and details differ from platform to platform, and almost no other site mentions the business.

Can I ask AI not to recommend certain competitors?

No, and you don't need to. What you can do is make your own information easier for AI to find and understand. A more useful step is to look at which websites the AI cites when it recommends competitors, and decide whether you should be on those sites too.
