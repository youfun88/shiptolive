Why does GEO take time?

Before an AI assistant recommends you, several things have to happen, and each one takes time:

  • Your website has to be re-read. After you change your site, the crawlers from Google, Bing and the AI companies have to come back and read it again. New pages also have to be indexed.
  • The sources AI uses have to update. AI assistants that search the web read what is online. New content has to be found by search engines before it can be cited.
  • Outside information has to build up. Your Google Business Profile, industry directories, reviews and mentions on other websites all take time to establish.

So visibility in search and AI builds up over time, usually over several months. Be careful with anyone who says you will be "guaranteed a recommendation in a few days". Results also depend a lot on you: the more completely you answer questions and the faster you review drafts, the faster things improve.

How does one round of optimization work?

Once you see how one round works, it is clear why results build step by step instead of arriving all at once. A complete approach usually repeats five steps:

  1. Monitor. Ask AI a fixed set of questions, and check your website and how it performs in Google search.
  2. Analyze. Find the questions where AI doesn't mention you, anything AI gets wrong, and search terms you could win.
  3. Suggest. Turn each opportunity into a specific change, with the reason behind it.
  4. Review. You check that the content is correct. It goes live only after you approve it.
  5. Publish and track. Update the website, wait for search engines and AI to read it again, then measure again.

Each round starts from the results of the last one. The first round is often about the basics, such as making sure AI crawlers can read the site and adding missing service descriptions. Later rounds work on getting mentioned for more questions. So if the numbers barely move in the first month or two, that doesn't mean nothing is happening. Look at early signs instead: is website health improving, and are more pages indexed?

Because it is a loop, how fast you respond sets the pace. The sooner you review drafts and answer questions, the sooner the next round can start.

Which 6 metrics should you track?

  • How often AI answers mention you. Out of the same set of questions, how many answers name your business. This is the most direct GEO metric.
  • How often AI cites your website. How many answers list your website as a source. Being cited means the AI read your content and trusted it.
  • Each platform on its own. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity give different answers, so record them separately.
  • Google AI Overviews. Whether your important searches show an AI Overview, and whether it cites you. This is an AEO metric. See how to show up in Google AI Overviews.
  • Google search performance. Clicks, impressions and your top search terms, from Google Search Console.
  • Where inquiries and bookings come from. In the end it is about business: for each booking or form, did the customer come from Google, from AI, or from somewhere else?

If you are still comparing agencies, ask each one how it reports these 6 metrics.

How do you measure accurately?

AI answers can change every time, so how you measure matters more than any single result:

  • Fixed questions: pick the questions your customers ask most as your optimization targets, and ask the same set every time.
  • Fixed schedule: check on a regular schedule, so you don't only count the good days.
  • Keep records: save each raw answer, who it named and which websites it cited, so you can compare.
  • Watch the trend: ups and downs between single checks are normal. Look at the direction over several months.

FAQ

How long does GEO take to show results?

Usually months. After you change your website, search engines and AI assistants need time to read it again, and information about your business on other sites takes time to build. Check with the same set of questions on a regular schedule and look at the trend over several months.

How do I know if GEO is working?

Track how often AI answers mention you, how often they cite your website, whether Google AI Overviews cite you, and where your inquiries and bookings come from. Use the same set of questions every time so you can compare before and after.

Why did AI mention me this time but not the next?

AI answers change. The same question asked twice can get two different answers. That is why you check on a fixed schedule and look at the trend across many results, not at one answer.

Can I make GEO work faster?

Yes, mostly through how quickly you can help: send the facts only you know, review drafts quickly, and complete outside listings such as your Google Business Profile. Fixing technical problems on your website first also helps new content get read sooner.
