How does ChatGPT decide who to recommend?

When ChatGPT needs current information, it searches the web first, reads a few relevant websites and then writes an answer from them. So a recommendation is not an ad slot you can buy. It is the AI's judgment after reading what it found. It tends to recommend three kinds of businesses: ones whose websites it can read, ones that answer the question clearly, and ones that other websites also mention. What are 7 practical steps to get recommended?

  • Make sure ChatGPT can read your website. OpenAI says ChatGPT search uses a crawler called OAI-SearchBot, and sites that block it in robots.txt won't appear in ChatGPT search answers. Check your website and CDN settings.
  • List the questions your customers ask. Use their words, such as "interior designer for a small apartment" or "who renovates small spaces near me", not your own marketing language.
  • Give each service its own clear page. Say in the first paragraph what you offer, which areas you serve, which customers it suits, roughly how it works and how to get in touch.
  • Write your common questions as Q&A. Use the question as the heading and answer it in the first sentence. See how to write an FAQ that AI will cite.
  • Look after your outside listings. Complete your Google Business Profile, industry directories and customer reviews, with the same name, address, phone number and website everywhere.
  • Give others a reason to mention you. Useful how-to articles, case stories, press coverage and introductions from partners all become sources that AI reads.
  • Check with the same questions on a schedule. Once a month, ask ChatGPT and other AI assistants, and record whether they mention you, who they recommend and which websites they cite. See how to check whether ChatGPT and Gemini recommend your business.

Why do different AI assistants recommend different businesses?

We often use this example when we brief clients: ask ChatGPT and Claude "What's the best Chinese restaurant in Taipei?" The two give different lists, and both name only a few places. For a business owner, that means two things:

  • Checking one AI isn't enough. If ChatGPT recommends you, that doesn't mean Gemini or Claude will.
  • The list is short, so the gap is big. The few businesses on it go straight onto the customer's shortlist. The ones left off may never be noticed.

So when you check, ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity the same set of questions. Then you know which platform to work on first.

What does a service page that AI can understand look like?

Before AI can recommend you, it has to be able to confirm a few things from your website. In our AI checks, when an assistant names a business it usually cites that business's own website, so these answers belong on your own site. When we rewrite service pages for clients, we make sure the page answers at least these 6 questions:

What AI wants to knowWhat the page should say
Who you areFull company name, brand name, year founded, city
What you doSpecific services, not adjectives
Where you serveYour service area, for example one city, a region, the whole country or online
Who you are forYour main types of customers, their size or situation
How to work with youThe process, the timeline and how pricing works
How to contact youForm, email or booking link, matching what other platforms show

This looks basic, but many websites are vague about it. If a page only says "professional team, dedicated service", AI can't tell whether you fit what the customer asked for, such as a city, "small apartment" or "on a budget". Being specific is what gets you picked.

Finally, use one company name everywhere. If your website uses one name, your Google Business Profile another and an old directory listing a third, AI may treat them as different businesses. This is common when a company has changed its name or uses names in two languages. A large share of AI answers cite directories and review sites rather than company websites, so keeping those listings consistent with your site matters.

FAQ

How does ChatGPT decide which businesses to recommend?

When search is on, ChatGPT searches the web in real time, reads what it finds and writes an answer from it. It tends to recommend businesses whose websites it can read, whose services are described clearly, and that other websites also mention. The list can change with the time and the wording of the question.

How do I make sure ChatGPT can read my website?

According to OpenAI, ChatGPT search uses a crawler called OAI-SearchBot, and sites that block it will not appear in ChatGPT search answers. Check that your robots.txt file and your hosting and CDN settings do not block it.

Will blocking GPTBot stop ChatGPT from recommending me?

According to OpenAI, GPTBot is used for model training and has nothing to do with whether you appear in ChatGPT search results; OAI-SearchBot decides that. So you can choose whether to allow GPTBot, but don't block OAI-SearchBot.

Can I pay to have ChatGPT recommend me?

No, you can't buy a recommendation inside an answer. ChatGPT is testing ads in some countries, but OpenAI says ads are labeled as sponsored, kept separate from the answer, and don't change what the answer says. No one can guarantee a recommendation either. What you can do is make your website readable, your content clear and your outside listings consistent, then check for changes with the same set of questions on a regular schedule.
