What does AI search optimization do for a small business?

When someone asks ChatGPT "who's a good property manager in San Diego?" or Google shows an AI Overview for "best estate lawyer near me", the answer names a few businesses and links a few sources. AI search optimization (GEO and AEO) works on two things: whether AI assistants name your business in those answers, and whether they cite your own website as the source.

Ship to Live does this with our own system, the AI Visibility Engine. It asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity the questions your customers ask, records who gets recommended and which sites are cited, and turns what's missing into concrete fixes on your website: clear answers to those questions, accurate service and location details, and structured data that AI can read.

How does the monthly check-and-fix loop work?

AI answers change, so one fix isn't enough. Each month runs the same five steps, and each round starts from the last one's results:

  1. Check. Once a month, the AI Visibility Engine asks the AI platforms in your plan your customers' questions, always the same set of questions, so the numbers are comparable over time. Every day it checks your website and handles your reviews, and on Grow and Scale it publishes the changes you approved.
  2. Analyze. We list the questions where AI doesn't name you, the sites it cites instead, and anything it gets wrong about you.
  3. Suggest. Each fix comes with the reason behind it, tied to your optimization goals.
  4. You approve. Drafts go to your review page. Nothing that changes your site's content goes live without your OK.
  5. Publish and track. We publish the changes you approve (automatic publishing is part of most plans). Your dashboard shows what changed in the next checks.
