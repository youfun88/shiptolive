Why is there no standard price for GEO?

Because every business tracks different questions, works on different platforms and starts from a different website. A company that only wants ChatGPT to recommend it, and whose website is already in good shape, needs far less work than one with no website that has to cover both English and Chinese markets.

So before you hear a price, ask "what does this price include?" The 6 factors below are where GEO quotes most often differ.

Where does the money usually go?

Knowing what GEO work involves each month makes it easier to judge a quote. A complete approach repeats five steps, and each round starts from the last one's results:

  1. Monitor. Check website health and speed, Google rankings and clicks, and ask AI your customers' questions, recording which answers name you or cite your website.
  2. Analyze. Turn the results into opportunities: questions where AI doesn't name you, keywords within reach, pages close to the top, and things AI gets wrong.
  3. Suggest. Propose specific changes for your goals, each with a reason.
  4. Review. Drafts go to you, and they go live only with your OK.
  5. Publish and track. Update the website, keep tracking what changes, and report regularly.

The biggest labor costs are usually writing content (step 3) and analysis (step 2). Monitoring can be automated, so an agency with its own system can spend more of your budget on content and analysis. When you ask for a quote, ask how much of each step happens every month, and which parts a system does and which a person does.

How do you compare GEO quotes?

When you have a few quotes, put them on one sheet before comparing prices:

  • Questions and platforms: how many questions does each track, and which AI platforms?
  • Checks and reports: how often do they check? Can you see every result? How often do they report?
  • Content and review: how much content each month? Who approves it before it goes live?
  • Beyond your website: do they cover your Google Business Profile, directories and other listings?
  • Contract: how long, and what can be adjusted?

A much lower price usually means a smaller scope, for example checking only, with no content, or traditional SEO only. That isn't necessarily bad, as long as it covers what you need. Before you sign, ask each agency how it measures whether AI names you and who approves changes to your site. More questions to ask are in how to choose a GEO agency.
