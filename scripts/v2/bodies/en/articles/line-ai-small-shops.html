Which small shops is a LINE AI chatbot good for?

A LINE AI chatbot does one simple job. A customer sends a message, and the AI answers right away from the information you gave it. When something needs your judgement, it tells you to take over. So it helps in two situations:

  • You answer the same question several times a day. Opening hours, days off, prices, how to book, where to park. The answer never changes, but you still type it out by hand every time.
  • Customers ask when you're busy. Many people message in the evening, when they finally have time. You may be closing up, prepping for tomorrow or with your family. If you reply the next day, they may already have asked the shop down the street.

Bakeries, cafes, hair and nail salons, tutoring centers, studios and local services (cleaning, repairs, moving) usually fit at least one of these, as long as their customers already message them on LINE. LINE is the everyday messaging app in places like Taiwan, Japan and Thailand. If your customers don't use LINE, this isn't the channel to start with.

Self-check: 5 questions to see if you need it

Open LINE Official Account Manager, look at last month's chats, and answer these 5 questions:

QuestionIf the answer is yes
1. Were more than half of last month's messages about things with a fixed answer, like hours, prices, booking or your address?The AI can take over those questions.
2. Did a good number of messages arrive after 9 pm or on your days off?The AI gives those customers an answer on the spot.
3. Have you ever replied a day late, or missed a message, because you were too busy?The ones you miss are often people who were about to buy.
4. Can you write the answers down (a price list, booking rules, a cancellation policy)?The AI has something to answer from. If you can't write them yet, sort that out before launch.
5. Do you have LINE messages to answer almost every day?Repeat questions add up to more time than you think.

Three or more yeses: a LINE AI chatbot will probably help. One or two: start with LINE's free keyword auto-replies and set up the two or three questions you get most. This checklist is only to help you think it through, not a hard rule. If every message takes you a long time to answer, it can be worth it with fewer messages too.

When should you wait?

  • You get very few messages. If it's only a few a week, answer them yourself, or use keyword auto-replies for "address" and "hours".
  • Every reply is custom. For example, every made-to-order piece needs photos and a back-and-forth before you can quote. All the AI can do is say "Got it, the owner will reply", which doesn't help much.
  • Your answers aren't settled yet. If prices keep changing or the rules are still in flux, settle them first. Otherwise the AI will keep answering from old information.
  • You have only a few fixed questions and want to do it yourself. The AI chatbot built into LINE Official Accounts (beta, a NT$100 subscription, as LINE words it) may be enough. For how the options differ, read how to choose and set up a LINE AI chatbot.

What must always go to a person?

A LINE AI chatbot isn't there to replace you. It answers the fixed questions first, so the ones that need your judgement reach you faster. During setup we list these cases with you. When the AI meets one, it tells the customer the owner has been notified and emails you the conversation:

  • Quotes and large orders: for example, a company ordering 80 snack boxes, a private booking or a group class.
  • Complaints and refunds: the AI apologizes, asks for the order details, and you handle it.
  • Safety questions: for example, allergies. The AI only repeats what your menu says, never promises "no peanuts", and asks the customer to check with staff.
  • Anything not in your information: the AI doesn't guess, and it doesn't promise prices, discounts or dates.
  • A customer who says "I want a person."

You can take over any time, from the LINE Official Account app on your phone or from chat.line.biz on a computer.

FAQ

Should a small shop use a LINE AI chatbot?

Look at three things: do customers keep asking the same few questions, do people message in the evening and on days off, and is there anything you must handle yourself. If the first two are yes, and the AI can hand the third kind to you, it's worth it. If you get only a few messages a week, or every reply has to be custom, LINE's free keyword auto-replies are enough.

How much does a LINE AI chatbot cost a small shop each month?

If you only use your LINE Official Account to answer customers, most businesses stay on LINE's free plan. For the AI, LINE's built-in AI chatbot is a NT$100 subscription in Taiwan. Ship to Live's plans are Starter $39 a month (no setup fee), Light $79 a month and Standard $159 a month, billed monthly with no contract. See plans and prices for setup fees and what each plan includes.

What if the AI gives a wrong answer?

The AI answers only from the Q&A, website and files you approved. It doesn't promise prices, discounts or dates that aren't in your information, and it hands questions it isn't sure of to you. We test it with real questions before launch, and after launch we read real chats and add the questions it answered poorly. You can always take over in the LINE Official Account app.

I run the shop by myself and I'm not technical. Can I still use it?

Yes. On Starter you follow a wizard and we suggest Q&A from your website. If you'd rather not do it yourself, on Light and Standard we write the Q&A and test it with you before launch. If you don't have a LINE Official Account yet, we can apply for one for you.

Is there a contract?

No contract on Starter, Light or Standard. They're billed monthly and renew automatically, and you can cancel any time; cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for. The Light and Standard setup fee is charged once, with your first payment.

My business is in the US. Can I use it?

Yes. You sign up for a LINE Official Account with a LINE Business ID, which you can create with an email address. For a business registered in the United States, LINE doesn't sell its paid plans there, so the account stays on the free plan with a small monthly limit on broadcast messages. Our AI still works fully: it answers with LINE's free reply messages, and hand-offs reach you by email.
