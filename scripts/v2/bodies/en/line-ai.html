How we set it up: four steps to launch

  1. Set up your LINE Official Account

    We apply for and set up the account: name, profile photo, welcome message and hours. Already have one? We connect to it.

  2. Build your AI knowledge base

  3. Test it with you on real questions

What do you need to prepare?

The AI is only as good as the information you give it. You don't have to start from scratch: we can draft the first Q&A from your website, menu, price list or past LINE chats, and you check it.

Checklist: the 8 things we ask you for
  • Common questions and standard answers (Q&A)

    What customers ask most, and how you want each one answered.

  • Services and prices

    Only what you are happy to publish. Mark anything that needs a custom quote as a hand-off.

  • Hours and address

    Including days off, directions and parking, the details customers ask about.

  • How to book or buy

    How customers book or order, and what they need to bring.

  • Return and cancellation policy

    Your rules for rescheduling, cancelling and refunds.

  • Cases that must go to a person

    For example quotes, complaints, medical or legal questions that need a professional, and special requests.

  • Brand tone

    For example how formal to be, whether to use emoji, and how short answers should be.

  • Things the AI must not say

    Promises you can't make, details you don't share, and topics you don't want the AI to bring up.

What the AI does, and what it won't do

The AI handles the common questions well and passes anything that needs judgment to you. You can step in at any time.

✓What the AI does

  • Answers common questions in seconds, at night and on holidays too.
  • Replies in the language the customer uses.
  • Answers only from the public information you provide.
  • For quotes, contracts, payments, complaints, or a customer saying "I want to talk to a person", it tells the customer the owner has been notified and emails you the conversation; you take over from the LINE Official Account app or chat.line.biz.
  • Logs new conversations as leads you can see in the admin dashboard.

✕What the AI won't do

  • Ask customers for passwords, card numbers or ID numbers.
  • Promise anything that isn't in your information, such as prices, discounts or delivery dates.
  • Guess when it isn't sure. It passes the question to you.
  • Run up costs on a flood of messages. Usage limits kick in when volume suddenly spikes.

FAQ

Still have questions? Add us on LINE and ask the AI, or leave a message below. We reply within 1–2 business days. Choosing a provider? Read the full setup guide.

Do I need a LINE Official Account already?

No. If you don't have one, we apply for it and set it up. If you already have one, we connect to it. You don't need to open a new account.

Will the AI make things up?

The AI answers only from your AI knowledge base: your Q&A, website, files and live data, all information you give it and are happy to make public. For each message the system looks up only the matching parts, and when sources disagree, the Q&A you approved wins. It doesn't promise anything that isn't in that information. When it isn't sure, it passes the question to you instead of guessing. Before launch we test it with you on real questions. It can still give a weak answer now and then, so after launch we read real conversations every month and add the questions it handled poorly to your Q&A.

What happens when a customer wants a person? Can I still reply myself?

When a customer asks for a person, or asks about a quote, contract, payment or complaint, the AI tells them the owner has been notified and sends you an email with the conversation. You can reply yourself at any time, from the LINE Official Account app on your phone or from chat.line.biz on a computer.

Starter, Light or Standard: which should I choose?

Two things decide it: how many different customers message you each month, and who sets it up. Up to 100 and happy to do it yourself: Starter ($39 a month, no setup fee). Up to 300, or you want us to write the Q&A with you and test it: Light ($79 a month). Up to 1,000, many questions, more monthly tune-ups or replies in your own tone: Standard ($159 a month). Over 1,000 or several people handling customers: Enterprise (from $319 a month). You can move up a plan later.

How much Q&A do I need to prepare?

You don't need to write it all at once. The questions customers ask most are enough to launch. We can also draft the first version from your website, menu, price list or past LINE chats. After that we add to it every month based on real conversations.

Can the AI reply in my own tone?

Yes, on Standard and Enterprise. We collect 20–50 replies you have written before (LINE, email or Facebook messages all work) and turn them into a writing guide: how you address customers, the phrases you use, emoji, reply length, and how you say no politely. The AI checks these examples on every reply, and we keep adjusting the guide each month from your real replies. No separate model training is needed. Light has basic tone settings (formal or friendly).

Can the AI check stock or open booking slots?

Yes. Light, Standard and Enterprise include live data from a Google Sheet, for example stock, prices or seats, with no setup or monthly fee; Starter doesn't. Connecting an online-shop, POS or booking system, or your own database, is an add-on: online-shop, POS or booking systems from $549 setup (+$29 a month), custom systems or databases quoted after review; all add-on prices. Lookups are read-only and limited to the fields needed. The AI never touches customer records, places orders or takes payments. If the system doesn't respond, the AI asks the owner to confirm.

How often does the Google Sheet sync?

There is no fixed schedule. The sheet is read again at three moments: when you press Save and sync or Sync now, and when a customer asks a question and the saved copy is more than 10 minutes old (the AI reads the latest sheet before answering). So an edit takes effect within 10 minutes at most, or right away if you press Sync now. If Google cannot be reached, data under 30 minutes old is still used and the customer is told what time it is from; anything older, the AI does not guess and hands the chat to you. If seats or stock change fast, press Sync now after a big edit.

What access do you need? Does the account stay mine?

The account stays yours, and you never give us a password. In LINE Official Account Manager, open the permissions page under Settings, create an invite link and add us as an admin. With that access we turn on the Messaging API, connect the AI, and set up the provider in your company's name so the account stays under your name. You can remove us from the same permissions page at any time, and the AI stops replying.

Is my data safe?

The AI only has the information you are happy to make public. It never asks customers for passwords, card numbers or ID numbers, and it doesn't promise anything that isn't in your information. If messages suddenly spike, usage limits keep costs under control. Within 90 days after cancellation takes effect we delete your conversations and AI knowledge base; before that you can ask for an export or earlier deletion.

Do I pay LINE anything extra?

The AI answers customer messages with LINE's free reply messages, so replies cost nothing extra. LINE's monthly plans are mostly about how many broadcast and push messages you send. If you only use the account to answer customers, you usually don't need to upgrade. If you want to send broadcasts, we'll walk you through the cost for your volume.

What counts as a monthly active contact? Does the AI stop past the limit?

A monthly active contact is a different person who messaged you this month. A customer who writes 10 times counts once, and months follow Taipei time. Going past the included number never stops the AI: Light adds $15 for each extra 100 contacts, Standard adds $10 and Enterprise adds $7; a part of 100 counts as a full 100 (301 contacts on Light is one extra block). You never pay more than the next plan's monthly fee: Light tops out at $159 a month (the Standard price) and Standard at $319 (the Enterprise price). No contract on Starter, Light or Standard. They're billed monthly and renew automatically, and you can cancel any time; cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for. The AI keeps answering until then, and you aren't charged again. The Light and Standard setup fee is charged once, with your first payment. Enterprise is a 12-month contract. My business is in the US. Can I get a LINE Official Account?

Yes. You sign up with a LINE Business ID, which you can create with an email address, so you don't need a Taiwan phone number. LINE records the country or region of the business that runs the account, and that decides what's available. For a business registered in the United States, LINE doesn't sell its paid plans there, so the account stays on the free plan with a small monthly limit on broadcast messages, and the verified blue badge is only issued in a few markets such as Japan, Taiwan and Thailand. Our AI still works fully: it answers with LINE's free reply messages, and hand-offs reach you by email. If you have a company or partner in Taiwan, registering the account there gives you LINE's full set of plans. We'll check the right setup with you before we start.
