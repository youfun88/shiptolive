What do you need to prepare?

The AI is only as good as the information you give it. This is what we ask for. You don't have to start from scratch: we can draft the first Q&A from your website, menu, price list or past LINE chats, and you check it.

  • Common questions and standard answers (Q&A)

    What customers ask most, and how you want each one answered.

  • Services and prices

    Only what you are happy to publish. Mark anything that needs a custom quote as a hand-off.

  • Hours and address

    Including days off, directions and parking, the details customers ask about.

  • How to book or buy

    How customers book or order, and what they need to bring.

  • Return and cancellation policy

    Your rules for rescheduling, cancelling and refunds.

  • Cases that must go to a person

    For example quotes, complaints, medical or legal questions that need a professional, and special requests.

  • Brand tone

    For example how formal to be, whether to use emoji, and how short answers should be.

  • Things the AI must not say

    Promises you can't make, details you don't share, and topics you don't want the AI to bring up.

What the AI does, and what it won't do

The AI handles the common questions well and passes anything that needs judgment to you. You can step in at any time.

✓What the AI does

  • Answers common questions in seconds, at night and on holidays too.
  • Replies in the language the customer uses.
  • Answers only from the public information you provide.
  • For quotes, contracts, payments, complaints, or a customer saying "I want to talk to a person", it tells the customer the owner has been notified and emails you.
  • Logs new conversations as leads you can see in the admin dashboard.

✕What the AI won't do

  • Ask customers for passwords, card numbers or ID numbers.
  • Promise anything that isn't in your information, such as prices, discounts or delivery dates.
  • Guess when it isn't sure. It passes the question to you.
  • Run up costs on a flood of messages. Usage limits kick in when volume suddenly spikes.

FAQ

Still have questions? Add us on LINE and ask the AI, or leave a message below. We reply within 1–2 business days.

Do I need a LINE Official Account already?

No. If you don't have one, we apply for it and set it up. If you already have one, we connect to it. You don't need to open a new account.

Will the AI make things up?

We set the AI to answer only from the public information you give it, and it doesn't promise anything that isn't in that information. When it isn't sure, it passes the question to you instead of guessing. Before launch we test it with you on real questions. It can still give a weak answer now and then, so after launch we read real conversations every month and add the questions it handled poorly to your Q&A.

What happens when a customer wants a person?

When a customer asks for a person, or asks about a quote, contract, payment or complaint, the AI tells them the owner has been notified and sends you an email with the conversation. You reply to the customer on LINE.

Can I still reply myself?

Yes, any time. Reply from the LINE Official Account app on your phone, or from chat.line.biz on a computer.

How much Q&A do I need to prepare?

You don't need to write it all at once. The questions customers ask most are enough to launch. We can also draft the first version from your website, menu, price list or past LINE chats. After that we add to it every month based on real conversations.

Can the AI reply in my own tone?

Yes. On the Standard plan we collect 20–50 replies you have written before (LINE, email or Facebook messages all work) and turn them into a writing guide: how you address customers, the phrases you use, emoji, reply length, and how you say no politely. The AI checks these examples on every reply, and we keep adjusting the guide each month from your real replies. No separate model training is needed.

Can the AI check stock or open booking slots?

Yes, as an add-on. We give the AI a read-only lookup, for example product stock or open booking slots. The data can come from a Google Sheet, a POS or online-shop system, or your own database. The AI reads only the fields it needs, never touches customer records, and never places orders or takes payments. If the system doesn't respond, the AI asks the owner to confirm. Pricing: Google Sheets from NT$8,000 setup (+NT$500 a month), online-shop, POS or booking systems from NT$18,000 setup (+NT$1,000 a month), custom systems quoted after review.

What access do you need? Does the account stay mine?

The account stays yours, and you never give us a password. In LINE Official Account Manager, open the permissions page under Settings, create an invite link and add us as an admin. With that access we turn on the Messaging API, connect the AI, and set up the provider in your company's name so the account stays under your name. You can remove us from the same permissions page at any time, and the AI stops replying.

Is my data safe?

The AI only has the information you are happy to make public. It never asks customers for passwords, card numbers or ID numbers, and it doesn't promise anything that isn't in your information. If messages suddenly spike, usage limits keep costs under control.

Do I pay LINE anything extra?

The AI answers customer messages with LINE's free reply messages, so replies cost nothing extra. LINE's monthly plans are mostly about how many broadcast and push messages you send. If you only use the account to answer customers, you usually don't need to upgrade. If you want to send broadcasts, we'll walk you through the cost for your volume. Our own fees are NT$2,490 a month for Light and NT$4,900 a month for Standard, before tax.
