What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO improves your ranking in Google Search. AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your content cited in answers such as Google AI Overviews. GEO (generative engine optimization) raises the chance that ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI tools recommend you. They work best together.

Why not just ask ChatGPT myself?

A single chat is shaped by your name, location and chat history, so you don't see what customers see. The engine uses a fresh chat and the same set of questions every time, in Chinese and English, so results can be compared over time.

How do I get my company into Google's AI Overviews?

The page first has to be indexed by Google and eligible to show a search snippet. After that it comes down to normal SEO basics and content that answers customers' questions directly. Google says no special AI file or markup is needed. Start by letting Google crawl the site, linking your service pages so they are easy to find, putting key information in text, and giving each question its own direct answer.

How can a small business show up more in AI search?

Pick the 5 questions customers ask most and check whether ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity mention you, who they recommend and which sites they cite. Then give each question a direct answer on your site (what you offer, where, for whom, how pricing works, how to contact you), make your name, address, phone and website the same on your Google Business Profile and other listings, and re-check with the same questions every month.

Crawlers are blocked or pages aren't indexed, key content is only in images, service descriptions are too vague to answer specific questions, the business name and details differ from platform to platform, and almost no other site mentions the business. What does AEO mean?

AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your content cited in answers such as Google AI Overviews. It shares its basics with SEO and GEO; the goal is what differs. SEO aims for a higher ranking in Google Search, AEO aims to be cited in answers, and GEO aims to be recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI tools.
