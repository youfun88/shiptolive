HOW AI DECIDES

How does AI decide who to recommend?

what are the most fun escape rooms in Taipei?

When a customer asks an AI “what are the most fun escape rooms in Taipei?”, the AI reads a few dozen websites on the spot and writes two or three names into its answer. Its criteria are plainer than they sound: it can read your site, your pages answer the question clearly, and other places mention you too. That is what SEO, AEO and GEO cover:

SEOSearch engine optimization
Rank higher in regular Google search.
AEOAnswer engine optimization
Get your pages quoted as a source in Google’s AI Overviews and AI Mode.
GEOGenerative engine optimization
Get recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI assistants.

Clear, correct content makes you easier to find, cite and recommend. That brings more visits, and visits turn into bookings and inquiries. It only works if it keeps running, so we built a system that does: the Ship to Live AI Visibility Engine.

FAQ

Questions

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO improves your ranking in Google Search. AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your content cited in answers such as Google AI Overviews. GEO (generative engine optimization) raises the chance that ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI tools recommend you. They work best together.

Why not just ask ChatGPT myself?

A single chat is shaped by your name, location and chat history, so you don't see what customers see. The engine uses a fresh chat and the same set of questions every time, in Chinese and English, so results can be compared over time.

Will my website content change without my approval?

No. Any change that publishes content on your site goes to you for approval first.

Can you guarantee AI will recommend us?

No one can guarantee that a given platform will recommend you or that rankings will rise. We measure, find the reasons, improve your content, and track the change in your dashboard.

Send your website and main city. We reply with a check report within 1–2 business days.
