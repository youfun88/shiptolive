    • Replies in your language

      The AI answers in the language you write in. Ask in English and it answers in English, even if the Q&A is in Chinese. The demo's own notes on LINE are in English when your code comes from this page.

    • Kept for 24 hours only

      What you enter and the chat are used only for this demo. After 24 hours the code stops working and the data is deleted. We don't collect names, phone numbers or emails.

    • Please use made-up details

      This is a public demo. Don't enter personal information, passwords or anything you want to keep private.

    • Hand-off is only shown

      When the AI hands a chat to a person in the demo, it shows a note that the owner would be notified. No email is sent.

    • Usage limits

      To prevent abuse, each network can generate, start and ask only a limited number of times per hour.

    • The AI can still get things wrong

      It answers only from what you entered, but an answer can still miss. After launch, we read real chats every month and add to your Q&A.
