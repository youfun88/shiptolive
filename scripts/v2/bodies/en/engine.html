Something else? Ask us

What is the AI Visibility Engine?

The AI Visibility Engine is an AI search analysis system built in-house by Ship to Live. It asks ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI assistants the questions your customers ask, records which answers mention you and which competitors they recommend, and lists what to improve on your site first.

How is it different from a regular SEO tool?

A regular SEO tool tracks Google rankings. The AI Visibility Engine also asks AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity, and records who they name and which sites they cite.

How often does it check?

Every day it re-checks AI answers, Google search and site health. AI checks run up to 3 times a day with the same set of questions, so the numbers are comparable over time.

Will the system change my website on its own?

No. Any change that publishes content on your site goes to you for approval first.
