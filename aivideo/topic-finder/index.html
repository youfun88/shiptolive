Find a promo topic people already want

Tell it what you sell and the video you're thinking of. It pulls what people actually type into YouTube and Google, opens the six places to find proven topics, and scores your options so you film the one with demand behind it. 對標選題, in one page.

1. Your product and idea

Say what you sell and the video you want to make, for example: 「我賣登山接駁，想拍玉山新手的影片」. Tap again when you're done.

2. What people actually search

Live suggestions from YouTube and Google autocomplete for your product and idea. These are real phrases people type. Tap one to add it to your shortlist.

    3. Check the six places

    Each link opens a ready-made search for your top ideas. Look for the same topic doing well for several creators, not one lucky video.

    4. Score your shortlist

    Tap Get YouTube numbers on a candidate to pull real views, subscribers and upload dates; it ticks the first three checks for you. Tick the rest yourself. Three or more ticks means it's worth filming, and the best one is marked.

    Suggestions come straight from Google's public autocomplete. View counts come from the YouTube Data API, cached for a day; there's a daily limit on fresh lookups. Your shortlist is saved in this browser only. Voice input uses your browser's built-in speech recognition (Chrome sends the audio to Google to transcribe it); nothing is recorded or kept by this page.
