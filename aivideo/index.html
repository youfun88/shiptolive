The daily 2 hours

Same shape every day, so you spend the time making, not deciding what to do.

20 minLearn: one technique, one tutorial or reference video
80 minMake: generate, compare, iterate
20 minLog: best prompt, what failed, credits spent

The prompt formula you'll practice all month

Subject+Action+Setting+Camera+Lighting+Style+Audio 
A tired night-shift nurse in her 30s leans against a vending machine,
staring at her phone and slowly smiling. Empty hospital corridor at 3am.
Slow dolly-in from medium shot to close-up. Cool fluorescent light with
a warm glow from the phone screen. Realistic, 35mm film look, shallow
depth of field. Audio: faint fluorescent hum, distant monitor beeps,
she whispers "finally."
