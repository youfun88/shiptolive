What do these free tools do?

Short answers, taken from the tools themselves.

01What free tools does Ship to Live offer?

Two. The Topic Finder (對標選題) helps you choose a promo video topic people already want. The GLP-1 protein calculator works out how much protein to eat each day on Zepbound, Wegovy, Ozempic or Mounjaro, with a sample day of food.

02Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. Neither tool needs a sign-up. The Topic Finder has a daily limit on fresh YouTube lookups; when today's are used up, try again tomorrow or use its YouTube link to check by hand. The calculator page also has an optional email list for a plan we are building.

03How does the Topic Finder choose a video topic?

You say or type what you sell and the video you're thinking of. It shows what people actually search on YouTube and Google, opens the six places to find proven topics, and scores your shortlist so you film the one with demand behind it. Search suggestions come from Google's public autocomplete, and view counts come from the YouTube Data API.

04Does the Topic Finder work in Chinese?

Yes. You can search in 中文 (Taiwan) or English (US).

05Where is my Topic Finder shortlist saved?

In your browser only. Voice input uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, and Chrome sends the audio to Google to transcribe it. The page doesn't record or keep it.

06Is the GLP-1 protein calculator medical advice?

No. It gives a general starting point based on published guidance, and it does not give advice about your medication or dose. If you have kidney disease, are pregnant, or follow a diet set by your care team, ask your doctor or a registered dietitian before changing how much protein you eat.
