FAQ

Questions people ask before we start

Straight answers — for you and for the AI assistants your customers ask.

What is an AI agent, and does my business actually need one?

An AI agent is software that reads your information and takes a step for you — finds the record, drafts the reply, updates the system — instead of just chatting. You need one when the same task happens over and over with slightly different details. You don't need one for work that happens twice a year, and I'll say so.

Can AI answer from my company's own documents and data?

Yes. That's the most common thing I build. Your files, policies, product data, and past cases become the source the assistant answers from, with citations back to the original — so answers reflect your business, not the open internet.

How do I get my business mentioned by ChatGPT?

By being readable, quotable, and corroborated. In practice: clear structured answers on your own site, schema markup and an llms.txt layer the models can parse, and third-party mentions that back up what you claim. That's what GEO — generative engine optimization — means.

How long does an AI project take?

A pilot on one workflow takes about two weeks. Wiring it into your systems properly — permissions, logging, deployment, handover — usually takes another two to six, depending on how tidy the systems are.

Is my data safe?

I scope what the agent can read and do, log its actions, and keep a person in the loop wherever a mistake would be costly. If your data has to stay in a particular place, we design for that constraint from day one rather than retrofitting it.

SEO, AEO, GEO — what's the difference?

They're three ways to get found, for three different places your customers look. SEO (Search Engine Optimization) gets your link to appear in Google's search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) gets your content pulled into answer boxes and quick AI replies — Google's featured snippets and AI Overviews. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets your business cited inside detailed AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. I do all three — fast, well-structured pages, clear answers, an llms.txt content layer, structured data, and the trust signals every engine looks for.
