FAQ

Questions before we start

What services does Ship to Live offer?

AI agents and assistants, workflow automation and integrations, mobile apps (iPhone, Android, Mac), websites and web platforms, search visibility (SEO, AEO and GEO), and rescuing or modernizing stalled and aging projects. Each project has one accountable lead from architecture to deployment.

How does a project start, and how is it priced?

With a free conversation about what you need. Then you get a short written plan with a fixed price, quoted in one email. Larger AI projects start with a two-week pilot on one workflow, running on your real data.

Can an AI agent answer from my company's own data? Is that safe?

Yes. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) makes your documents, policies and records the source the assistant answers from, with citations back to the original. We build RAG and agent systems, and our AI Visibility Checker uses the same approach. We set what the agent can read and do, log every action, and keep a person in the loop wherever a mistake would be costly.

SEO, AEO, GEO: what's the difference?

SEO gets your link into Google and Bing results. AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your content into featured snippets and AI Overviews. GEO (generative engine optimization) gets your business cited in answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. All three rest on fast pages, clear answers, structured data and an llms.txt layer. Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Google will recommend us?

No, and nobody honestly can. AI engines decide whom to name, and they change often. What we can do is make your site easy to find, check and cite in English and 中文, fix what keeps you out, and measure how often you're named before and after, with the same questions each time.
