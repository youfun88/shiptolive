Google Trends check

What the search data says about the ideas

Pulled from Google Trends on October 11, 2026: US web searches, October 2021 to now. It confirmed the home idea, narrowed it to mold and water damage, and showed GLP-1 is bigger than we assumed.

Google Trends shows relative interest, not search counts, and 2026 covers January to October only. Some rising searches for broad terms like "home repair" are local ads and quiz questions, so we only quote the rising searches that are clearly real people.

The ideas

Three things worth testing, best first

Picked for how quickly they could reach people through search, not just how big the market is.

01Fastest growing

Snap a photo, find out what's wrong at home

Take a photo of a leaking water heater, a mold spot, or a part you can't name. The app tells you what it is, how serious it is, roughly what it costs to fix, and whether to do it yourself or call a pro.

Start with mold and water damage, the two problems with the fastest-growing searches, and answer the health worry first. Then grow into free web pages, one per problem, and a paid home-maintenance tracker with reminders.

  • House & Home
  • Photo search
  • SEO pages
  • Web first, then app

Why it fits

  • Fastest-growing category in the data
  • Google searches for "water damage" are up 222% and "mold in house" up 65% since 2021
  • Rides the shift to searching by photo (25B+ Lens searches a month)
  • Few AI apps here yet
  • Every problem is its own search, so each page can bring in Google traffic
  • The maintenance tracker gives people a reason to come back
02Testing now

A GLP-1 companion for muscle and life after the drug

About 1 in 9 US adults now takes a GLP-1 like Zepbound or Wegovy. There are already plenty of apps for logging shots. The two problems they mostly leave alone: 15–45% of the weight lost can be lean mass, including muscle, and in one major trial people regained about two-thirds of it within a year of stopping.

So the app focuses on those: a daily protein target, short strength workouts, and a 12-week plan for coming off the drug. The first test is a free protein calculator with a waitlist for that plan. 100+ sign-ups in 2–3 weeks means we build it.

  • Health & Fitness
  • Subscription
  • Daily habit
  • Live test
Try the protein calculator →

Why it fits

  • Searches for "zepbound" rose from 4 to 87 since 2023, more than double "home repair"
  • Record $4.5B in-app spending; people already pay
  • Daily tracking builds the habit AI apps lack
  • Users gather in big, easy-to-find communities (r/Zepbound alone has about 211k weekly visitors)
03Fast but small

Hundreds of niche calculator and converter pages

Generate a page for each specific question in one niche. It's the quickest way to get Google traffic, but each visitor is worth little, so it works best as a way to bring people to idea 01 or 02.

  • Utility
  • SEO
  • Ships in days

Why it fits

  • People search these when they need an answer right now
  • Cheap to build on our existing web stack

Skipped on purpose: general chatbots (big players win), short-drama video apps (need a content studio and ad budget), and finance (regulation and trust slow everything down).

Before building

Check demand in one afternoon

  1. Google Trends

    Look up the idea's search terms with the "Rising" filter on. Are they growing?

  2. App Store search

    Type the keywords and note what autocomplete suggests. Read the 1–3 star reviews of the top 3 apps.

  3. Reddit

    Count how often the same question comes up each week in r/HomeImprovement or r/fixit.

  4. Ship one page

    Put up a single working page and see whether people find it and use it before building the app.
