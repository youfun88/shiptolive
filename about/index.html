About

You work with the engineer, not an account manager

Ship to Live is a founder-led software studio in San Diego and Taipei. We build AI agents, automations, mobile apps and websites for businesses in the US and Taiwan, and we take each one all the way to production. We bring in specialists when a project needs them.

Why "Ship to Live"

"Ship to live" is the last step of a build: putting the code into production, where people use it. Plenty of projects stall before they get there. That last step is the one we care about most.

So every engagement starts by finding what's actually in the way, whether that's a missing integration, an aging system or an unclear plan, and ends with software running on your real data, with logging, permissions and handover docs. You own the code and the accounts.

We keep teams lean on purpose. One accountable lead owns each project from architecture to deployment, and we bring in specialists when a project needs them.
