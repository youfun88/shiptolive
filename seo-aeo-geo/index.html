Three kinds of search

One customer, three places to look

The same groundwork serves all three: a fast site, pages that answer real customer questions, structured data, an llms.txt file, and listings that agree with each other across the web.

  • SEO: rank in search results

    Your pages appear in Google and Bing when someone searches for what you sell. Most buying searches still start here, and many AI engines read the same results before they answer.

  • AEO: be the quoted answer

    Google lifts a short answer from your page into a featured snippet or an AI Overview. That takes the customer's question on the page, a direct answer under it, and markup that tells Google what it is.

  • GEO: get named by AI assistants

    Someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Claude who to hire, and your business is one of the names. These engines draw on your site and on the directories, reviews and articles that mention you.

  • English and 中文, checked separately

    AI engines often name different businesses in each language and each city. We check and write for both languages, from the cities where your customers are.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us

How long until we see results?

Several AI engines search the web live, so fixes to your site and listings can show up in their answers within weeks. Google rankings for competitive searches usually take three to six months. We report every month, so you can see what is moving either way.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Google will recommend us?

No one controls what Google ranks or what an AI engine says, and AI answers change from run to run. We guarantee the work, agreed in writing, and measure it with the same questions before and after, so you can judge the results yourself.

Do we need a new website?

Usually not. Most fixes work on the site you already have, including WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and custom sites. We recommend a rebuild only when the platform blocks the basics: speed, structured data or access for AI crawlers.

Who writes the content?

We do, in English and Traditional Chinese. Every draft goes to a private review page that Google and AI can't see, and you click Approve or Request changes. Nothing goes live without your OK, since you know your customers and your business best.

What is in the free AI visibility check?

About ten questions your customers would ask, sent to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude, with who gets named and the top fixes for your site. It is free while the AI Visibility Checker is in early access.

What do I get each month?

A plain-language report email in English or 中文, a short to-do list for next month, and a login to your own results page: who AI names for your customers' questions, whether Google's AI summary cites you, your Google rankings and clicks, Microsoft Copilot citations, leads from your site, and site health.

What are Google AI Overviews and AI Mode?

An AI Overview is the AI-written summary Google often shows above the regular links. AI Mode is Google's chat-style search. Both cite a few websites. For your important searches we check whether yours is one of them, and who is cited instead.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO gets your pages into Google and Bing results. AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your answers into featured snippets and AI Overviews. GEO (generative engine optimization) gets your business named in answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Copilot. All three rest on fast pages, clear answers, structured data, an llms.txt file and consistent listings.
