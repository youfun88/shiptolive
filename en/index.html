FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Websites & Digital Marketing

Can you redesign our site without rebuilding it?

Yes. We first review your current site's design, speed, mobile experience and search performance, then recommend which parts to redesign.

Do you build bilingual or multilingual sites?

Yes. We build English, Chinese and other language versions, with the language tags search engines need, so customers in each market land on the right page.

Custom Software Development

How do you price software projects?

We agree on scope, approach, timeline and cost before any work starts, and quote each project on its own. If a tool you already have can solve the problem, there's no need to build everything from scratch.

Can you take over an existing system?

Yes. We maintain, upgrade and improve existing systems, so you don't have to rebuild every time something needs to change.

AI Search Optimization (AEO/GEO)

What do you need for a free AI visibility check?

Your website and the main city you serve. Optionally, the 3 questions customers ask most and up to 3 competitors. Within 1 to 2 business days you get a report: whether AI mentions you, which competitors it recommends, and what to fix first.

Can you guarantee AI will recommend us?

No one can guarantee that a particular platform will recommend you or that rankings will rise. What we do is measure regularly, find the reasons, improve your site's content, and track the changes in your dashboard.
