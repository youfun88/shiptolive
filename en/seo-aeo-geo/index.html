How does AI decide who to recommend?

When a customer asks an AI “which lawyers in Taipei handle cross-border inheritance?”, the AI reads a few dozen websites on the spot and writes two or three names into its answer. Its criteria are plainer than they sound: it can read your site, your pages answer the question clearly, and other places mention you too.

All three are things you can do. The site's technical side has to be easy for search engines and AI to read. Pages have to answer the questions customers actually ask, in English and in Chinese. Listings, reviews and your own site have to agree with each other. Once those are in place, Google and AI both find it easier to recommend you.

We don't guess. Every month we ask four AI engines the same set of customer questions and count who they name and whose site they cite. If the numbers go up, it worked.

What owners ask.

01What is tech marketing?

Getting your business named and recommended when customers look for what you do, on Google and in AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. We fix the site's foundations, write pages that answer customers' questions directly, keep listings consistent, and measure every month with the same set of questions to see whether AI names you more often.

02How long until we see results?

AI engines search the web live, so fixes to your site and listings can show up in their answers within weeks. WU LAW INTL PLLC went from 0 to 26 AI answers citing the site four days after the first check. Google rankings for competitive searches usually take three to six months. You get numbers every month, so you can see what is moving either way.

03Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Google will recommend us?

No one controls what Google ranks or what an AI engine says, and AI answers change from run to run. We guarantee the work, agreed in writing, and measure it with the same questions before and after, so you can judge the results yourself.

04Do we need a new website?

Usually not. Most fixes work on the site you already have, including WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and custom sites. We recommend a rebuild only when the platform blocks the basics: speed, structured data or access for AI crawlers.

05Who writes the content, and can anything go live without my approval?

We write it, in English and Traditional Chinese. Every draft goes to a private review page that Google and AI can't see, and you tap Approve or Request changes on your phone. Nothing goes live without your OK.

06What do I see each month?

A login to your own dashboard, any time: how often AI names you for your customers' questions, how you compare with peers, Google ranking distribution and clicks, Microsoft Copilot citations, bookings and inquiries from your site, and site health. Plus a plain-language report email and next month's to-do list.

07What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO gets your pages into Google and Bing results. AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your answers into featured snippets and AI Overviews. GEO (generative engine optimization) gets your business named in answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Copilot. All three rest on fast pages, clear answers, structured data, an llms.txt file and consistent listings. We call the whole thing tech marketing.
