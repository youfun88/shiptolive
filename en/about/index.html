AboutFounded in Taiwan, 2022

A Taipei tech company that gets things live.

夏裕科技 (Xiayu Technology) was founded in Taiwan in 2022. We are headquartered in Taipei and serve clients in Taiwan and the US. We do three things: tech marketing, custom software development and business AI adoption. Each one starts with a diagnosis and ends with something live.

Our rule: it has to go live

“Live” is the last step of any project: in production, where customers use it and where Google and AI can find it. Plenty of projects stall before they get there. That last step is the one we care about most.

So every engagement starts by finding what’s actually in the way, whether that’s a missing integration, an aging system or an unclear plan, and ends with software running on your real data, with logging, permissions and handover docs. You own the code and the accounts.

We run on our own systems: the Xiayu AI Visibility Engine, a results report every client can open, and a review step before anything goes live. One lead owns each project from start to finish.
