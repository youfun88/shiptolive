ABOUT SHIP TO LIVE

About Ship to Live

Solid engineering and new ideas, so good plans actually ship.

Ship to Live (夏裕科技股份有限公司) was founded in 2022. We're headquartered in Taipei and serve clients in Taiwan and the US. We focus on custom software, business system integration, websites, digital marketing and AI search optimization.

We bring together software engineering, AI, system architecture, visual design and digital marketing. From planning through development to launch, we help companies improve how they work, get more done and grow online.

IN-HOUSE R&D

We build our own products, too

Besides building websites and business systems for clients, we keep investing in our own software products. We design, build and maintain them ourselves, from architecture and code to data integration and cloud deployment.

Our lead product, the Ship to Live AI Visibility Engine, is an AI search analysis system we built in-house. It combines brand visibility checks, competitor analysis and website improvement tracking, so a business can see how it shows up in AI search tools like ChatGPT and Gemini.

What we learn building our own products goes straight into client work, so the solutions fit real needs.
