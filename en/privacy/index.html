Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Xiayu Technology Co., Ltd. (夏裕科技股份有限公司, tax ID 90173045, "we") collects on shiptolive.com and on our client dashboard admin.shiptolive.com, how we use it, and how you can ask us to see or delete it.

What we collect

  • Browsing the site: we use Google Analytics to see how the site is used (pages visited, device type, approximate region). It uses cookies. We don't use it to identify individuals.
  • Contact and booking: when you use the contact form or book a call on Calendly, we receive the name, email, company and message you provide.
  • Client dashboard sign-in: when a client signs in to admin.shiptolive.com with Google, we receive only your name and email address, to check which site's reports you may see. We don't read your Gmail, Drive or any other Google account data.
  • Website data clients authorize: with a client's permission, we read that website's Google Search Console and similar performance data to prepare reports and improve the site.
  • Contract signing: when a contract is signed online, we keep the company details, the plan, the signature and seal images, and the signing time, to form and keep the contract.

How we use it

Only to provide and improve our services: replying to you, scheduling meetings, client reports, signing and keeping contracts, invoicing and billing. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Services we use

Data is stored with or passes through these providers: Google (Analytics, Sign-In, Search Console), Calendly (booking), Vercel and GitHub Pages (hosting), Supabase (database) and Brevo (email delivery). They process data only on our instructions.

Google user data

Data we receive through Google Sign-In (name and email) is used only for signing in and access control. Our use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't share it with third parties or use it for advertising.

Retention and your rights

Contract and billing records are kept for the period the law requires; other data is deleted when no longer needed. You can ask to see, correct, stop the use of, or delete your personal data by emailing yufan@shiptolive.com. We'll reply within a reasonable time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and update the date above.
