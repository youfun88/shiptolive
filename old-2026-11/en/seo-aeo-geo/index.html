How does AI decide who to recommend?

which lawyers in Taipei handle cross-border inheritance?

When a customer asks an AI “which lawyers in Taipei handle cross-border inheritance?”, the AI reads a few dozen websites on the spot and writes two or three names into its answer. Its criteria are plainer than they sound: it can read your site, your pages answer the question clearly, and other places mention you too.

All three are in your hands. That is what SEO, AEO and GEO cover:

SEO
Rank higher in regular Google search.
AEO
Get your pages quoted as a source in Google’s AI Overviews and AI Mode.
GEO
Get recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI assistants.

Clear, correct content makes you easier to find, cite and recommend. That brings more visits, and visits turn into bookings and inquiries. It only works if it keeps running, so we built a system that does: the Xiayu AI Visibility Engine.

01

Monitor

Every day the engine checks your site’s health and speed, your Google rankings and clicks, and how you compare with the competitors you choose. It also asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity your customers’ questions and records which answers name you and cite your site: every day (up to 3 times a day), the same questions each time. It also records which page each booking or form came from, without names or emails.

WU LAW INTL PLLC. First check on October 26: 0 of 45 answers cited the site. October 30: 26.

02

Analyze

It sorts what it finds into four kinds of opportunity: questions where AI names someone else, searches people make that aren’t crowded yet, pages ranked 4 to 10 that a small edit can lift, and sentences AI gets wrong about you. It also looks beyond your site: whether your Google Business Profile, Bing Places, LinkedIn and industry directories agree with each other, and which sites AI actually cites, which often points to new places to be listed. Each one becomes a specific fix.

Search terms on Google page 1 went from 29 to 48, and top-3 terms from 5 to 18. October 30 → November 6.

How we check AI answers

AI answers vary from run to run, so the method stays fixed. That is what makes this month comparable with last month.

Real customer questions
The questions your customers actually ask, without your company’s name. Put your name in the question and AI almost always says something nice. The real test is whether it recommends you when nobody mentions you.
English and 中文
The same questions in both languages. Plenty of businesses show up in one and vanish in the other.
Fresh chats, live search
A new conversation every time, with no personal history, and each assistant’s live web search turned on, so the answers match what customers see.
From your customers’ city
Questions are asked as if from where your customers are, such as Taipei or San Diego. “Near me” and “best” questions change with location.
More than one run
Each question runs several times and we report the share of answers that name you, never a single answer.
Same set, steady schedule
Your goals are asked on every check. AI checks run every day (up to 3 times a day); your site and Google data are checked daily too.
Raw sources
Google numbers come from Google Search Console and Copilot citations from Bing Webmaster Tools. Every AI answer is saved, so you can read exactly what it said.

08What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked in Google and Bing results. AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your pages quoted as a source in Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. GEO (generative engine optimization) gets your business recommended in answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Copilot. All three rest on fast pages, clear answers, structured data, an llms.txt file and consistent listings. We call the whole thing tech marketing.

09How do I know whether AI mentions us?

Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity the questions customers ask, without your name in them: fresh chats, live search on, several runs per question, and count the share of answers that name you. Asking ChatGPT once yourself usually misleads, because your name is in the question and the answer is tailored to your location and history. The free check does this properly, once. How we check

10What if AI says something wrong about us?

We compare every sentence AI writes about you with your site and sort them into “contradicts your site” and “not supported by your site”. Contradictions get fixed on your site and on listings like your Google Business Profile. Unsupported claims we check with you first, then write the correct version clearly. AI searches the web live, so the next check usually shows the change.

11Do we need llms.txt? Are FAQ and structured data enough?

llms.txt is a short file on your site written for AI to read. It’s cheap and worth having, but it isn’t the deciding step. FAQ and structured data are groundwork too, not the whole job. Who AI recommends also depends on whether it can read your site, whether your answers are clear, and what other places say about you: your Google Business Profile, industry directories, reviews and press. The Xiayu AI Visibility Engine checks all three.

12We’re small and in a niche field. Does this suit us?

Yes. It suits any business whose customers ask AI before they choose. In a niche field the questions are more specific, and a site that answers them clearly is easier to cite. Start with the free check to see how AI answers your customers today.

Your company name, website and the city your customers are in; if you can, the 3 questions they ask most and up to 3 competitors. No budget talk needed. 14Can we pay to have ChatGPT recommend us?

Who AI recommends in its answers isn’t for sale. We work on the recommendations AI writes on its own: making sure it can read your site, understand your services and find you mentioned elsewhere. Advertising is a separate thing and not part of our service.

15How should we handle forums and online reviews?

AI reads online discussions and reviews, so reputation matters. We keep your Google Business Profile and listings consistent, remind you to ask happy customers for genuine reviews, and answer the questions people ask on forums clearly on your site. We don’t post fake reviews or write paid word-of-mouth posts.
