About us

Before we start

01What does 夏裕科技 (Xiayu Technology) do?

Three things: tech marketing (so Google and AI assistants like ChatGPT find you and name you, run on our AI visibility engine), custom software (turning your processes into a system or app you own), and AI adoption (handing repetitive work to AI with automations your team approves). We were founded in Taiwan in 2022, are headquartered in Taipei, and serve clients in Taiwan and the US. Every engagement starts with a diagnosis and ends with something live.

02Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Google will recommend us?

What we commit to: steady checks, evidence behind every change, and monthly numbers you can compare with where you started. We ask AI the same customer questions on every check and fix what leaves you out. AI answers can shift within weeks; Google rankings usually take months.

03Will anything go live without my approval?

No. Drafts wait on a private review page on your site. It isn't public, and you sign in with Gmail. Nothing is published until you tap Approve, and you can tap Request changes instead. Technical SEO items like titles and structured data are reviewed by us and don't change what you say.

04What do I send for the free check, and what do I get back?

Your company name and website, and the city your customers are in. If you can, add the 3 questions customers ask most and up to 3 competitors to compare. We put about ten customer questions to ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity and email you a report within 1–2 business days: which answers name you, who AI recommends instead, which sites it cites, and what to fix first. No budget talk needed.

05We’re a small business in a niche field. Is this for us?

Yes. It suits any business whose customers ask AI before they choose: cross-border professional firms, travel and bookings, training and B2B services, local shops and experiences. The more niche the field, the more specific the questions, and the easier it is for a site with clear answers to get cited. Start with the free check to see how AI answers your customers today.

06How are software projects priced, and can we stop halfway?

We start with discovery (1–2 weeks, fixed fee), which ends with a written plan, a clickable prototype and a fixed quote; the plan is yours even if you stop there. After that we ship a working version every two weeks, and you can stop after any phase. Software and AI work is quoted per project.

07Will adopting AI leak our company data?

We agree up front what the AI can read and what it can do. Every action is logged, and anything that costs money or goes out to customers needs a person to approve it. Adoption also includes drafting your company's AI usage policy.
