## 4. Proposed Ship to Live offer ladder: 「AI 導入」

Positioning line (draft): **「讓每位員工都有一個 AI 助理——從一個流程開始，看得到省下的時間。」**
Alt: 「不是教你用 ChatGPT，是幫你的團隊把每週重複的工作交給 AI。」

| Tier | Offer | Format | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Free | YouTube (繁中, "做給你看＋範本免費拿"), 電子報, **LINE 社群/官方帳號**, free templates (prompt 範本、n8n workflow JSON、Claude Skills), free 60–90 min 線上體驗課 monthly | Content plus live webinar | Free |
| 1 Individuals | 「AI 工作流程實戰班」: small online cohort, 4 weeks × 2 hr live plus homework. Each participant ships one automation of their own work. | Online cohort with replay and community | TBD |
| 1b Individuals | Self-paced recorded course and template pack, as the downsell | Recorded | TBD |
| 2 Corporate | **員工 AI 工作流程培訓 (企業內訓)**: 半日 intro / 全日 hands-on / 4–6 週 陪跑 program (workshop, then each team ships one workflow, then a demo day) | On-site in TW or online, 10–40 people | TBD |
| 3 Done-for-you | **AI 自動化代建**: discovery (流程盤點), then build n8n / Claude agent workflows, then handover docs and training | Project | TBD |
| 4 Ongoing | **AI 顧問月費**: maintenance, new workflows each month, office hours, internal AI usage policy updates | Retainer | TBD |

Flow: the free template and 體驗課 lead to the LINE list. Individuals in the cohort bring it to their boss, which leads to 企業內訓. In each training, the 流程盤點 surfaces automation candidates, which become 代建 projects and then 月費. This mirrors Nate Herk's ladder adapted to TW habits: LINE instead of Skool, live 體驗課 like 雷蒙's, and a subsidy angle for corporate deals.

### Sample before/after workflows (**illustrative examples only, not client results**)
1. **週報 / 月報彙整 (行政、業務主管)**
   - Before: open the CRM, Excel and LINE groups, copy numbers by hand, write the summary. About 2 hours/week.
   - After: an automation pulls the data every Friday, AI drafts the 週報 in the company template, and a person reviews and sends it. **About 10 minutes.**
2. **客服 / 詢價回覆 (電商、B2B 業務)**
   - Before: answer repetitive LINE / email / FB questions one by one (出貨、退換貨、規格). About 1–2 hours/day.
   - After: AI drafts replies from the FAQ, product sheet and order status. Staff approve or edit and send. Unusual cases go to a person. **About 20–30 minutes/day**, with consistent tone.
3. **會議記錄 → 報價單 / 待辦**
   - Before: write the meeting notes, then build the quote in Word/Excel, then chase follow-ups. About 1.5 hours per client meeting.
   - After: the recording is transcribed into a summary with decisions and to-dos. AI fills the quote template from the price list, and tasks are pushed to the team's tool. **About 15 minutes** of review.

(Any time figures used on the site must be labelled 「示意範例」 until real client data exists.)

### Corporate workshop curriculum (全日 version; half-day = modules 1–3 + 6)
1. **AI 現況與心法**: what genAI can and can't do, how a 「把 AI 當新進員工」 brief works, Taiwan data (84% already use it, mostly unmanaged)
2. **安全與規範**: what data may go in which tool, BYOAI risks, drafting the company's 「AI 使用守則」 (this addresses the 31% privacy concern)
3. **Prompt 到工作範本**: role, context, format and examples. Each attendee builds 3 reusable templates for their own job (email, summary, analysis)
4. **文件與資料處理**: summarizing long docs/PDFs, Excel analysis, meeting notes, 繁中 writing quality
5. **自動化入門**: connecting AI to tools (n8n / Zapier / Claude agents and Skills), live build of one workflow such as 週報 or 客服草稿
6. **流程盤點工作坊**: each team lists its repetitive tasks, scores them by time saved × feasibility × risk, and picks one to ship within 2 weeks
7. **導入計畫與成效衡量**: owner, pilot, hours-saved metric, 30-day follow-up (this leads into the 陪跑 / 代建 offer)

Deliverables to promise: a slide deck, a prompt template library, a 「AI 使用守則」 draft, each team's 流程盤點表, and a 30-day check-in.

### Landing-page structure suggestion (企業 AI 導入 page)
Pain (「員工各自用 AI、沒有規範、看不到成效」) → data strip (84% / 73% / 39%, cited) → before/after cards (示意) → offer ladder → curriculum → instructor/studio credibility (shipped apps, own automations) → 補助 note (carefully worded) → FAQ (資安? 不會寫程式可以嗎? 用哪些工具? 可以客製嗎? 可申請補助嗎?) → CTA 「預約 30 分鐘免費諮詢」 + LINE.
