Who gets named, and who doesn't

You're missing from the answer, a competitor gets cited, the AI reads a directory instead of your site, a page has no FAQ or structured data. Each gap maps to something we can fix, and goes on this month's to-do list.

Pages that answer the customer's question right away, FAQs, structured data (JSON-LD) and a site guide for AI (llms.txt), directory listings, in English and Chinese. Questions

Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Google will mention my business?

No. Nobody can guarantee an AI will mention you; we measure, we don't promise. Every month we ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini the same customer questions and record whether you're named and which sites they cite, so you can see what actually changed after each fix.

How soon will I see results?

AI answers can shift within weeks; Google rankings usually take months. The system re-checks your site every 8 hours and you get a plain-language report each month showing what we changed and which numbers moved.

How do you price software projects?

Quoted per project. Most start with a 1–2 week discovery (fixed fee) that ends with a written plan, a clickable prototype and a fixed quote. After that you get a working build every 2 weeks, and you can stop after any phase.

Who owns the code and accounts?

You do. Code, cloud accounts, domains and data are in your name from day one, with documentation and a handover. No lock-in.

Can staff who don't code take the AI training?

Yes. Training starts from each person's own work: reusable templates in tools they already have, then automating one repetitive workflow together. What the AI can read is scoped up front, actions are logged, and a person approves anything costly or external.
