Curriculum

What does the team training cover?

Suggested outline, adapted to your company The full day has seven modules; the half day is modules 1–3 plus 6. Every example uses your own work.

01Where AI is, and how to think about itHalf dayFull dayProgram

What generative AI is good and bad at, and how to brief it like a new hire. Most people already use it; most companies don't manage it.

You leave with: a "should this go to AI?" checklist.

02Safety and rulesHalf dayFull dayProgram

Which data may go into which tool, the risks of staff bringing their own AI tools, and how to write your AI usage policy.

You leave with: a draft AI usage policy.

03From prompts to work templatesHalf dayFull dayProgram

Role, context, format, examples. Everyone builds three reusable templates for their own job: emails, summaries, analysis.

You leave with: your own template library.

04Documents and dataFull dayProgram

Summarizing long documents and PDFs, Excel analysis, meeting notes, and getting natural writing in English and Chinese.

05Intro to automationFull dayProgram

Connecting AI to your tools (e.g. n8n, Zapier, Claude), with one workflow built live, such as a weekly report or reply drafts.

06Workflow inventory workshopHalf dayFull dayProgram

Each team lists its repetitive tasks, scores them on time saved × feasibility × risk, and picks one to ship within two weeks.

You leave with: your team's workflow inventory.

07Rollout plan and measuring resultsFull dayProgram

Who owns it, where to pilot, how to log hours saved; a check-in after 30 days.

When the training ends, you have

  • The slides and a prompt template library
  • A draft company AI usage policy
  • Each team's workflow inventory and first workflow to build
  • Program version: each team ships a working workflow, with a demo day at the end
  • A 30-day check-in

These are what the course is designed to deliver. How much time each team saves depends on the workflow; we measure it together.

How do you turn a process into software without it spiraling?

Start small, ship in phases, and make every phase usable. If an off-the-shelf tool does the job, we'll say so, and build only the part that's truly yours.

What we usually build: internal tools, booking and membership systems, client portals, iOS / Android apps, integrations and automation (CRM, LINE, ERP, retiring spreadsheets), AI assistants on your own documents, and rescues of stuck projects (old PHP too).

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Ship to Live do?

Three things: AI adoption (we teach teams to hand repetitive work to AI and build the automations), search visibility (we get you found on Google and named by AI assistants like ChatGPT), and custom software (we turn your process into a system). US work is contracted through QY LLC in San Diego; Taiwan work through 夏裕科技股份有限公司.

Do I need to code to take the AI training?

No. The half-day and full-day sessions need no coding. The focus is breaking your own work into steps AI does well, building reusable templates, and writing your company's AI usage policy. Automation is shown with no-code tools like n8n; anything more complex, we build for you.

Is it safe to give company data to AI?

We agree up front which data may go into which tool, and what the AI can read and do. Automations we build log every action, and anything that costs money or goes out to customers waits for a person to confirm. The training also helps you draft your own AI usage policy.

How soon will search visibility improve? Can you guarantee AI will mention us?

No one can guarantee that an AI will mention you; we measure, we don't promise. AI assistants that search the web live can change within weeks of fixing your site and listings; competitive Google rankings usually take months. We re-run the same questions every month so you can see the progress.

How do software projects start, and how are they priced?

Start with a free 20-minute call. Then a discovery phase (1–2 weeks, fixed fee) maps the process, decides what to buy versus build, and delivers a written plan, a clickable prototype and a fixed quote. After that you get a working version every two weeks and can stop after any phase. Software and AI work is quoted per project.
