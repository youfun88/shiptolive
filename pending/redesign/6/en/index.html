About us

Questions

Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Google will recommend us?

No. Nobody can guarantee an AI will mention you; we measure, we don't promise. Every month we ask four AI assistants the same customer questions, fix what makes you get left out, and show you the before-and-after numbers. AI answers can shift within weeks; Google rankings usually take months.

Will anything go live without my approval?

No. New pages and changes wait on a private review page that Google and AI can't see. Nothing is published until you tap Approve on your phone, and you can ask for changes instead.

How are software projects priced, and can we stop halfway?

We start with discovery (1–2 weeks, fixed fee), which ends with a written plan, a clickable prototype and a fixed quote; the plan is yours even if you stop there. After that we ship a working version every two weeks, and you can stop after any phase. Software and AI work is quoted per project.

Who owns the code and accounts?

You do. Code, cloud accounts, domains and data are in your name from day one, with documentation and a handover. No lock-in.

Will adopting AI leak our company data?

We agree up front what the AI can read and what it can do. Every action is logged, and anything that costs money or goes out to customers needs a person to approve it. Corporate training also includes drafting your company's AI usage policy.
