1Tech marketing · Google and AI search

Why doesn't AI search mention your business?

When customers ask ChatGPT or Google who to hire, the answer comes from a handful of sites. If AI can't read your site or find an answer worth quoting, it recommends someone else. Every month we measure how AI and Google describe you, fix the gaps weekly, and nothing goes live until you tap Approve on your phone.

The monthly loop, from audit to your approval

  1. We ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini, with live web search, the questions your customers really ask (15+ a month on the paid plan). We record who gets named and which sites they cite, and check Google's AI Overviews, Search Console, speed and structured data.

    4 AI enginesGoogle AI OverviewsGoogle search dataSite health

  2. Which questions don't name you, who gets recommended instead, which directories the AIs read; which pages lack an FAQ or structured data, or block AI crawlers in robots.txt. We also catch AI mixing you up with someone else.

  3. Pages that answer customer questions right away, FAQ, structured data for AI (JSON-LD, llms.txt) and directory listings, in English and Chinese. The system re-checks every 8 hours (02:00, 10:00, 18:00 Taiwan time) and queues the next fixes, so problems don't wait for the monthly report.

  4. Your dashboard charts the change: share of AI answers that name you, Copilot citations, page-1 searches and leads from the site. Every change is logged, so you can see what moved the numbers.

  5. Every change waits on a private review page and you get a notification. Nothing is published until you tap Approve; send it back if you don't like it. A plain-language report arrives each month, and you can log in any time.

About us

FAQ

Before you start, you might ask

Can you guarantee AI or Google will recommend my business?

No, and nobody can. What we do is measure, every month, how ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google describe you using the same customer questions, fix what's missing, and show you the before and after. AI answers can shift within weeks; Google rankings usually take months.

How is software development priced?

Quoted per project. We usually start with discovery (1–2 weeks, fixed fee), which delivers a written plan, a clickable prototype and a fixed quote. Then you get a working build every two weeks, and you can stop after any phase.

Who owns the system and the code?

You do. Code, cloud accounts, domains and data are in your name from day one, with handover documentation. You can have anyone maintain it later.

Can staff who don't code take the AI training?

Yes. The training starts from each person's own work: list the repetitive tasks, then turn them into reusable AI templates and workflows. Automations that need code, we build for you.

Is it safe to let AI touch company data?

What the AI may read is agreed up front, every action is logged, and anything that costs money or goes out to customers needs a person's approval. Training also includes a draft AI usage policy for your company.

Which company signs the contract?
