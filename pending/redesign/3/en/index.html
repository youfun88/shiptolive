01Tech marketing
Google and AI search, monthly

Get Google and AI to find you, and say your name.

Customers now ask ChatGPT “who’s good for…” before they search. Every month we ask too, measure what comes back, fix why you weren’t named, and nothing goes live until you tap approve on your phone.

Short answer

Why doesn’t AI search mention your business?

Usually not because you aren’t good, but because AI can’t read you or can’t make sense of you. No page that answers the customer’s question directly, no structured data, no listing on the directories AI reads, or English and Chinese names that don’t match: so the AI recommends someone it does understand.

The good news: most of this can be measured and fixed. The difference is whether someone asks, checks and fixes it the same way every month.

Every month

What do we actually do each month?

Five steps, repeated monthly. Every 8 hours (02:00, 10:00 and 18:00 Taiwan time) the system re-checks the site and drafts the next fixes, so problems don’t wait for the monthly report.

1

Audit

Ask the AIs, crawl the site

We take the questions your customers really ask (15+ on the paid plan) and put them to ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini with live web search on. We also crawl the whole site: speed, SSL, titles, structured data, llms.txt, and whether AI crawlers can get in.

02Software
Systems · apps · integrations · AI assistants

Turn your process into a system.

Orders still passed around in Excel? Bookings lost in LINE chats? The same data typed into three systems? We map the process first, buy off the shelf where it fits, and build only the part that is truly yours.

Short answer

How do you keep custom software from being a gamble?

Keep it small. Research keeps finding that the bigger the project, the more likely it runs over, runs late or ships features nobody uses. So we spend 1–2 weeks pinning down scope, then deliver in phases: a working version every two weeks, and you can stop after any phase. If off-the-shelf software does the job, we’ll say so and build only the part that is truly yours.

Honestly: does AI make development faster?

It depends. On well-defined new tasks, studies found 21% to 56% speed-ups7. But experienced developers working in their own large codebases were 19% slower with AI while believing they were 20% faster8.

So we use AI to get to a usable prototype sooner, and a senior engineer reviews every line, with automated tests run before delivery.

03AI adoption
Training · done-for-you automation · support

Teach your team to work with AI.

Not “how to use ChatGPT”. We find the work your team repeats every week, let AI draft it with a person checking, and you can see the time saved.

Short answer

Your staff already use AI. Why isn’t the company faster?

Because everyone uses it their own way: no shared method, no rules, and nobody turning what works into a team workflow. The biggest barrier is usually not money, it’s not knowing where to use it. We start by mapping workflows, get one working, then scale.

FAQ

Questions you might ask first

Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Google will recommend my business?

No, and nobody can. What we do is ask four AI assistants the same customer questions every month, track Google and Copilot citations, fix the gaps we find, and show you before-and-after numbers. AI answers can shift within weeks; Google rankings usually take months.

How long does a custom system take?

Discovery (1–2 weeks, fixed fee) pins down the process and scope and gives you a prototype and a fixed quote. Phase one typically takes 3–12 weeks depending on scope, with a working version every two weeks, and you can stop after any phase.

Who owns the code and accounts?

You do. Source code, cloud accounts, domains and data are in your name from day one, with documentation and a handover, so you can switch maintainers any time.

Can staff who don’t code take the AI training?

Yes. The training starts from each person’s own work: emails, meeting notes, weekly reports, customer replies. Everyone leaves with their own templates, and each team picks one workflow to automate.

Is it safe to give company data to AI?

We scope up front what the AI can read and do, every action is logged, and anything that costs money or goes out to customers needs a person to confirm it. Training includes a draft AI usage policy for your company.

Do you work in both Taiwan and the US?

Yes. Taiwan projects are contracted through 夏裕科技股份有限公司 (tax ID 90173045, with official Taiwan invoices); US projects through QY LLC in San Diego. We work in English and Chinese.
