FAQ

What do people ask about these projects?

Why do you build these AI agents for your own business first?

So each one runs on real data before a client relies on it.

What does the AI Visibility Checker do?

It asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude the questions your customers ask, in English and 中文, and shows whether your business gets named and who gets named instead. It is in development now. See how it works

Does the lead intake agent send replies on its own?

No. It reads each new inquiry, looks up the sender's company, matches the request to the right service and drafts a reply with a rough scope. Nothing is sent until a person approves it. This agent is still planned.

What will the AI assistant in Tally do?

You type something like “2 hours on the contract review yesterday” and Tally logs it, or drafts the invoice. This assistant is still planned.

Is the GLP-1 protein calculator free?

Yes. It is live, free and needs no sign-up. It shows how much protein to eat each day on Zepbound, Wegovy, Ozempic or Mounjaro, how much at each meal, and a sample day of food that gets you there. Open the calculator
