The problem

Your customers are asking AI who to hire

More people now skip the search results and ask an AI assistant: “Who's the best accountant in San Diego?” The assistant replies with two or three names. If yours isn't one of them, that customer won't reach your website, however good it is.

Most businesses don't know what these assistants say about them. Each engine searches its own way, the answers change from day to day, and English and Chinese results differ.

How it works

Four steps

  1. 1 You

    Tell us about the business

    Name, website, what you do and where. That's all it needs.

  2. 2 Ask

    Ask like a customer

    About ten real customer questions in English and 中文, sent to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude with live web search. Each one runs several times, because AI answers vary.

  3. 3 Read

    Read every answer

    Was your business named? In what position? Who was named instead, and which websites did the AI cite as its sources?

  4. 4 Check

    Check your website

    Structured data, an llms.txt file, whether AI crawlers are blocked, page speed: the things AI engines rely on to understand and trust a site.

What you get

A report you can act on

  • A visibility score: for each AI engine and each language, so you see where you're strong and where you're invisible.
  • The exact questions and answers: word for word, so you can read what each AI said.
  • Who gets named instead: the competitors AI recommends when it doesn't recommend you.
  • The sources AI trusts: the directories, review sites and articles it cites. That's where you need to appear.
  • Fixes, ranked by impact: specific changes to your site and listings, most important first.
  • A baseline to measure against: run it again after the fixes and see what changed.

Why it matters

Why it helps your business

  • See what customers see

    Know what the answer is when someone asks AI for a business like yours, before it costs you customers.

  • Know who you're up against

    AI may recommend businesses you didn't count as competitors. The report names them.

  • Fix the right things first

    You get a ranked list of changes for your site and listings, most important first.

  • Reach both markets

    Checks English and Chinese separately. Many businesses show up in one language and vanish in the other.

Under the hood

How it's built

Python and LangGraph, calling each engine's own web-search API so the answers match what real users see. Every response is saved, every run logs its cost, and we'll test the scoring against businesses whose results we already know.

  1. Ask one engine one question and save the answerDone
  2. Generate customer questions in English and 中文
  3. Add ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini in parallel
  4. Detect mentions, positions and competitors
  5. Check the website
  6. Score it and produce the report
  7. Test against known businesses; track cost
