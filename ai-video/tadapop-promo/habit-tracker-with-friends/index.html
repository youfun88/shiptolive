Habit tracker with friends: the Veo kit

Everything for a 45-second Tadapop promo with Chloe, our AI host: the first-frame image and the Veo prompt for every Chloe shot, the script, and the real app screens to record. Download a frame, copy its prompt, and generate.

Chloe is AI-generated. Turn on the platform's AI-generated content label when you post. She's a host, not a customer, so no "I lost 10 kg" style result claims.

How to use this in Veo

  1. In Google Flow (or Veo), start a new clip with Frames to Video and upload the shot's first frame.
  2. Paste the shot's Veo prompt. Set 9:16 and the length shown.
  3. Talking shots (lip-sync): make 2–3 takes and keep the ones whose voices match. Veo picks a new voice each time.
  4. Voice-over shots: keep the clip silent, and record the script as one voice-over track at the end so the voice matches throughout.
  5. If Veo changes her face, add the reference image below as an ingredient.
