How to read the results

  1. Sort by upload date as well as relevance. Videos from the last 12 months show current demand.
  2. Compare views to subscribers. Far more views than subscribers means the topic did the work, not the channel.
  3. Look for the same topic winning for several creators, not one lucky video.

Video ideas from the numbers

  1. "I did a 30-day challenge with my friends. Loser pays." Making this first
    The challenge trend (millions of views) plus Tadapop's friends-and-leaderboard twist. Works as 30天挑戰 in Chinese too.
  2. "75 Hard with friends: we tracked it with proof check-ins."
    Rides the 75 Hard trend.
  3. "The habit tracker you use with friends." A short demo for a phrase almost nobody owns yet.
  4. Get into the "best habit tracker app" roundups. Several of those reviewers are small channels that would likely answer an email.
